The short version: I'm Mohammed Safwan, a digital marketer from Palakkad, Kerala. I've worked in digital marketing since 2021, mostly for B2B service companies and healthcare brands in India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and other GCC countries.

I finished a BA in English and then took a digital marketing course at an academy. The course gave me the basics, but it wasn't enough to run real campaigns with real budgets. So I kept learning on the job.

Since then I've worked on almost every side of marketing: at an agency, in-house for a brand, and as a freelancer. Each one taught me something different. Agencies teach speed and range. In-house teaches you what the business actually cares about. Freelancing teaches you to own the result.

Some of the work I can show: SEO, Meta Ads and Google Ads for a multi-specialty hospital and a skin clinic in Bangalore (with the agency), Meta lead ads for a group of allergy and asthma clinics in Kerala, Google Ads for a cleaning company in Qatar, and SEO that took two Saudi manufacturers to the top of Google. The details and screenshots are in my case studies.

Today I work full-time with a digital marketing agency in Bangalore, remotely from Kerala. A big part of my work is healthcare marketing, where trust matters more than clicks and there are real rules to respect.
