Hi, I'm Safwan. I learn by doing, and I share what works.

S
Based in Kerala, India

I'm Mohammed Safwan, a freelance digital marketer from Kerala. For about four years I've been running Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO and social media for small and growing businesses.

I work directly with business owners, and with thinkB Creative Agency in Bangalore. My clients range from local businesses in Kerala to clinics and service companies in the UAE.

Over time, healthcare became a big part of my work. Marketing for doctors and clinics is different. Trust matters more than clicks, and there are real rules (like the NMC advertising guidelines) to respect. I enjoy getting that balance right.

I also share what I learn on LinkedIn and Instagram. Mostly real lessons from campaigns that worked, and a few that didn't.

How I work

  • You talk directly to me, not an account manager
  • Honest advice, even when it means spending less
  • Ad accounts stay in your name. You always own them
  • Tracking first, so decisions are based on real numbers
  • Reports in plain language, not jargon
