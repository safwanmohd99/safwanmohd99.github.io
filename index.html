FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

Are you a full-time freelancer?

No. I work full-time as a digital marketer with an agency in Bangalore. I take freelance consultations on the side, and full projects only occasionally when the fit is right.

What happens in a consultation?

A focused 1–2 hour call. I review your ads, website or marketing plan beforehand, then we go through what's working, what's wasting money and what to do next. You get clear, practical steps.

Do you work with businesses outside Palakkad?

Yes. I work with businesses across Kerala and India, and with brands in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and other GCC countries. Consultations happen over WhatsApp, calls and video meetings.

Which industries do you work with?

Mainly healthcare (clinics, hospitals, healthcare brands) and B2B service companies in India and the GCC.

Will I own my ad accounts and data?

Always. Accounts should be set up under your business, with partner access for anyone who helps you. If you stop working with someone, everything stays with you.
