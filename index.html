FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

What's the minimum ad budget I need?

It depends on your goal and city, but most small businesses can start testing with a modest daily budget. On our call I'll suggest a realistic number, and I'll tell you if it's too low to get useful results.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can start bringing enquiries within the first couple of weeks, though the first month is mainly about testing. SEO takes longer, usually a few months, but keeps working after you stop paying.

Do you work outside Kerala?

Yes. I work with clients across India and in the UAE. Everything runs over WhatsApp, calls and video meetings.

Do you only work with healthcare clients?

No. Healthcare is a big focus, but I also work with service businesses, real estate, education, retail and more.

Will I own my ad accounts and data?

Always. Accounts are set up under your business, and I get access as a partner. If we stop working together, everything stays with you.
