Allergy Asthma Foundation: ₹3.28 lakh revenue from ₹49.6K ad spend in one month

A network of outpatient allergy and asthma clinics across Kerala. No surgeries, no high-ticket procedures: just consultations and tests. That's what makes these numbers stand out.

The challenge

We started with click-to-WhatsApp ads. They brought plenty of chats, but too few turned into real patients, and the clinic teams spent time on enquiries that went nowhere.

What I changed

  • Switched to Meta lead forms, with a separate campaign for each clinic location
  • Added qualifying questions with conditional logic (who the visit is for, the health concern, when they plan to consult) to filter out low-intent leads before they reach the clinic
  • Tracked every lead with the clinic team, so budget moved to the locations and messages that brought real visits

The result

In September 2026, ₹49,634 in ad spend brought ₹3,28,100 in revenue across the clinics. In the first 10 days of October, ₹9,609 brought ₹1,21,600. All without surgical or high-ticket services.

VSH Hospital

Multi-specialty hospital · Bangalore 6 departments SEO, Meta Ads and Google Ads working together View case study

Full-funnel digital marketing for a multi-specialty hospital after its rebrand: search visibility, department campaigns and landing pages.

SEO & AI search

After the hospital's rebrand, I led SEO strategy and execution: a redesigned site structure, better appointment booking, and new sub-category pages so patients can find each speciality easily.

Content and keyword work focused on branded searches and high-value services. Over 12 months (October 2024 to September 2025), Google Search Console recorded 42.7K clicks and 1.64M impressions. The site ranked #1 for "colonoscopy checkup in bangalore", #3 for "colonoscopy cost in bangalore" and #5 for "knee replacement surgery cost in bangalore", and appeared in Google's AI Overviews.

Meta Ads

Department-wise campaigns for Orthopaedics (ACL surgery, knee replacement), Cardiology (heart check-ups around World Heart Day), Dermatology (GFC hair treatment), Urology (kidney stone care), Oncology (World Cancer Day screening) and Obstetrics & Gynaecology (PCOS care, mammograms, Women's Day).

Continuous creative testing, audience segmentation and A/B testing kept cost per enquiry under control in Bangalore's competitive healthcare market.

Google Ads

Search and Performance Max campaigns for Orthopaedics, Cardiology and Dermatology, built around high-intent keywords for specific treatments.

For example, a Performance Max campaign for Orthopaedics brought 13.4K clicks and 62 conversions for ₹10.2K, and a Search campaign for GFC hair treatment brought 10 conversions from 270 clicks.

I also planned and structured conversion-focused landing pages and tested versions to improve lead quality and cost per conversion.

Done as part of a digital marketing agency in Bangalore.
