How to start

Four steps, in this order.

I'm not against paid courses or academies. I just think they come later.

  1. Start with these free courses

    Pick one from the table below and finish it before you open the next.

  2. Understand the fundamentals

    Learn what SEO, paid ads, content and social media each do for a business, in simple terms.

  3. Find what you enjoy

    Notice which lessons you look forward to. That is usually the area to go deeper in.

  4. Then invest in a good paid course, if you need it

    Once you know your direction, a structured paid course is money well spent. You don't have to spend thousands of rupees just to find out whether digital marketing is right for you.

Questions

The straight answers.

Are these digital marketing courses really free?

Yes. All five are free to take on the official platforms. The one thing to know is Meta Blueprint: its courses are free, but Meta's separate certification exams may cost money. The sixth course on this page, from Senator We Run Ads, is paid and is clearly marked.

Do I get a certificate?

Google gives a badge for your Skillshop profile and LinkedIn when you complete the course. The two HubSpot courses and the Semrush course give a certificate after you pass the exam. Meta Blueprint courses are free to learn from, and its official certification exams are separate.

How long do the courses take?

Google's Fundamentals of Digital Marketing is estimated at 40 hours. HubSpot's Digital Marketing Certification is about 3 hours 24 minutes and its Content Marketing Certification is about 8 hours 23 minutes. Semrush and Meta Blueprint are self-paced, so check the course page for the current length.

Do I need any experience?

No. These are starting points, not advanced training. They are made for beginners who want to understand digital marketing and find out which part they enjoy.

Should I buy a paid course?

Not first. Start with the free ones, understand the fundamentals and see what you enjoy: SEO, paid ads, content or social media. If you then want more structured learning, a good paid course is worth it. You don't have to spend thousands of rupees just to find out whether digital marketing is right for you.
