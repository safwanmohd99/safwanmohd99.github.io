Why this matters

Clicks are not results.

If you're spending money on Google Ads, you need to know what happens after someone clicks.

01

See which ads bring real enquiries

Without conversion tracking, all you see is clicks and impressions. With it, you see which keywords, ads and campaigns lead to actual form submissions, phone calls or WhatsApp messages.

02

Smart Bidding needs conversion data

Google's automated bidding strategies like Maximize Conversions and Target CPA use your conversion data to decide how much to bid. Without it, they have nothing to optimise towards.

03

Stop wasting money on what doesn't work

When you can see that one campaign brings 20 enquiries and another brings zero, you know where to put your budget. Without tracking, you're guessing.

04

Prove your results

Whether you are a business owner or a marketer managing a client's account, conversion data is proof that the ads are working. It is the number your client or your boss actually cares about.

Before you start

What you need.

Tick these off before you begin.

What you'll set up

Two tags, one job.

Google Ads conversion tracking through GTM needs exactly two tags.

Tag 1 — set once

Conversion Linker

Fires on every page. When someone clicks your ad, the URL contains a click ID (called GCLID). The Conversion Linker captures this ID and stores it in a cookie. Without it, Google Ads cannot connect a conversion back to the click.

Tag 2 — per conversion

Conversion Tracking

Fires only when the conversion happens — a form submission, a WhatsApp click, a phone tap. It sends the conversion data to Google Ads using a Conversion ID and Conversion Label that you copy from Google Ads.

Quick summary: The Conversion Linker remembers who clicked your ad. The Conversion Tracking tag tells Google Ads that person did something valuable. You need both.
Step by step

From Google Ads to working tracking.

Follow these eight steps in order. The whole setup takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

  1. Create a conversion action in Google Ads

    Log in to your Google Ads account. In the left menu, go to Goals → Conversions → Summary. Click the blue + New conversion action button. Choose Website.

    Fill in the details:

    • Conversion name: something clear, like "Contact form submit" or "WhatsApp click"
    • Category: pick the closest match (Submit lead form, Contact, etc.)
    • Value: choose "Don't use a value" unless you know the value of each lead
    • Count: "One" for lead forms (one form = one lead, even if someone submits twice), "Every" for purchases

    Click Done, then Save and continue.

  2. Copy the Conversion ID and Conversion Label

    After saving, Google Ads asks how you want to install the tag. Choose Use Google Tag Manager.

    You will see two values:

    • Conversion ID — a number, like AW-123456789
    • Conversion Label — a string, like AbCdEfGhIjKlMn

    Copy both and keep them. You will paste them into GTM in step 4.

    Tip: The Conversion ID is the same for all conversion actions in your account. The Conversion Label is unique to each action. If you set up more conversions later, you only need the new Label.

  3. Create the Conversion Linker tag in GTM

    Open Google Tag Manager and go to your website's container. In the left menu, click Tags → New.

    • Name: type Conversion Linker
    • Tag Configuration: click it, then choose Conversion Linker from the list
    • Triggering: click it, then choose All Pages

    Click Save.

    This tag has no settings to fill in. It just runs on every page and captures the click ID from the URL. You set it up once and never touch it again.

  4. Create the Google Ads Conversion Tracking tag

    Still in GTM, go to Tags → New again.

    • Name: something descriptive, like Google Ads - Contact Form Submit
    • Tag Configuration: click it, then choose Google Ads Conversion Tracking
    • Conversion ID: paste the Conversion ID from step 2
    • Conversion Label: paste the Conversion Label from step 2

    Leave the other fields (Conversion Value, Transaction ID, etc.) empty for now unless you have specific values to send.

    Do not add a trigger yet. We will do that in the next step.

  5. Set up the trigger for the conversion event

    Still editing the tag from step 4, click Triggering. You need to tell GTM when this tag should fire. The two most common methods:

    Option A

    Thank-you page

    If your form redirects to a thank-you page after submission (like /thank-you/), create a trigger that fires when that page loads.

    • Click the + to create a new trigger
    • Name: Thank You Page
    • Trigger type: Page View
    • Choose: Some Page Views
    • Set: Page URL → contains → /thank-you/
    • Save the trigger
    Option B

    Button or link click

    If you are tracking a WhatsApp button click or a phone link click, create a trigger that fires when that specific element is clicked.

    • Click the + to create a new trigger
    • Name: WhatsApp Click (or similar)
    • Trigger type: Click - Just Links
    • Choose: Some Link Clicks
    • Set: Click URL → contains → wa.me
    • Save the trigger

    Select the trigger you just created and click Save on the tag.

    Important: The conversion tracking tag must fire only when the conversion happens, not on every page. If it fires on every page, Google Ads will count every visit as a conversion, and your data will be wrong.

  6. Preview and test with Tag Assistant

    Click the Preview button in the top right of your GTM workspace. Enter your website URL and click Connect. Two windows open: Tag Assistant and your website.

    Check two things:

    • Conversion Linker should show under "Tags Fired" on every page you visit. If it doesn't, check that its trigger is set to All Pages.
    • Google Ads Conversion Tracking tag should show under "Tags Not Fired" on normal pages. Now go to your website and trigger the conversion: submit the form or click the WhatsApp link. The tag should move to "Tags Fired" for that event.

    If both check out, your setup is correct.

  7. Publish the container

    Go back to your GTM workspace. Click Submit in the top right. Give the version a name like "Added Google Ads conversion tracking" and click Publish.

    Your tracking is now live on your website.

  8. Verify conversions in Google Ads

    Wait 24 to 48 hours after a real conversion happens on your site. Then go back to Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → Summary. Your conversion action should show a status of Recording conversions or Active.

    If the status still says "No recent conversions" or "Inactive" after 48 hours:

    • Go back to GTM Preview Mode and test again — make sure the tag fires
    • Check that the Conversion ID and Label are correct (no extra spaces)
    • Make sure the Conversion Linker tag is also firing on all pages
    • Try submitting a test conversion yourself and check again the next day
After setup

What to do next.

01

Wait for enough data

Smart Bidding strategies like Maximize Conversions need at least 15 to 30 conversions in the last 30 days before they can optimise well. Until then, use Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks.

02

Track more than one action

Repeat steps 1, 2, 4 and 5 for each conversion you want to track: form submissions, phone calls, WhatsApp clicks. Each gets its own Conversion Tracking tag and its own trigger. The Conversion Linker stays the same.

03

Mark primary and secondary

In Google Ads, mark your most important action as a Primary conversion (this is what Smart Bidding optimises towards). Mark less important ones as Secondary (they are tracked but do not affect bidding).

04

Check regularly

Open Goals → Conversions once a week to make sure the status is still "Active". If your website changes — a new form, a new thank-you page URL, a new theme — test the tracking again.

Questions

Common doubts.

What is the Conversion Linker tag?

It captures the click ID (called GCLID) from the URL when someone clicks your ad, and stores it in a first-party cookie on your website. Without it, Google Ads cannot connect a conversion back to the ad click that caused it.

How long before I see conversions in Google Ads?

Usually 24 to 48 hours for the first conversion to appear. Use GTM Preview Mode and Tag Assistant to verify the tag fires correctly right away; the delay is only on the Google Ads reporting side.

Can I track phone calls as conversions?

Yes, but it needs a different setup. Google Ads has its own call tracking with a forwarding number, or you can track clicks on a phone number link as a conversion event in GTM. The click-tracking method is simpler but only counts people who tap the number on your website, not people who dial it manually.

What is the difference between Conversion ID and Conversion Label?

The Conversion ID identifies your Google Ads account. It is the same for every conversion action in that account. The Conversion Label identifies the specific conversion action, like a form submission or a phone call. You need both values in the tracking tag.

Do I need a separate tag for each conversion action?

Yes. Each conversion action — form submit, phone call, WhatsApp click — needs its own Google Ads Conversion Tracking tag with its own Conversion Label. They all share the same Conversion ID. You only need one Conversion Linker tag for all of them.
