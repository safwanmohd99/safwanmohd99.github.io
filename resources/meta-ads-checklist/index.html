Why this matters

Your portfolio is a box.

Think of your Business Portfolio as a box. Your Page, Instagram account, ad account and Pixel are the things inside it. Each asset can sit in only one box, and whoever owns the box owns everything in it.

If a freelancer or agency created the box, you may still be able to post and run ads, but they are the owner, not you. That is how businesses end up with a Page they can't add to their own portfolio, or a budget they can't check.

The fix is simple if you do it on day one: you own the box, and you give the people who work for you a key, not the box.

If it's already happened

Your Page is owned by another portfolio?

Meta shows "This Page is already owned by another business" when you try to add it. Work through these steps in order.

  1. Find out which portfolio owns it

    Switch into your Page, then open Settings & privacy > Settings > Page setup > Page access. The owning portfolio's name is shown there. Meta will not show who the people behind it are.

  2. Ask the owner to remove it

    Contact the old agency, freelancer or ex-staff member. They go to Settings > Accounts > Pages, select your Page and click Remove. This is the fastest way, so try it first.

  3. Request a release

    If you have full control of the Page, you can send a release request from your side. The owning business gets around 30 days to respond. If nothing has changed after that, go to step 4.

  4. Contact Meta support

    If you can't reach the owner, contact Meta Business support with documents proving you own the business, for example your GST certificate, business registration or a utility bill.

    • Before a support-led release, disconnect the Page from any business Instagram account.
    • This can take time. Keep your case number and reply from the same email.

  5. Add the Page to your own portfolio

    Once it is released, add it from Settings > Accounts > Pages > Add. It doesn't move over by itself.

Questions

The straight answers.

Is Business Portfolio the same as Business Manager?

Yes. Meta renamed Business Manager to Business Portfolio. You manage it from Settings in Meta Business Suite at business.facebook.com.

My freelancer created the ad account in their portfolio. Can they transfer it to me?

No. An ad account's ownership cannot be transferred. It stays with the portfolio it was created in. They can share it with you, but the safer fix is to create a new ad account inside your own portfolio and run future ads from that one. The same is true for a Pixel (Dataset).

Should my freelancer or agency have full control?

Usually not. Add them as a partner from your own portfolio and share only the assets they need, with the lowest access that lets them do the work. Keep full control for yourself and one trusted person.

Do I need a Pixel if I don't have a website?

No. The Pixel only tracks what people do on your website. If your ads use Meta lead forms or send people to WhatsApp, you can skip setup 5.

Can I pay for Meta ads with UPI?

In India, Meta lets you add prepaid funds to your ad account using options such as UPI and NetBanking, or you can add a card. The payment options you see depend on your ad account. Whichever you use, it should be your own.
