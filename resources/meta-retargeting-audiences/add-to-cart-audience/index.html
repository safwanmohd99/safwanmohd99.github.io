Guide 6 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who added to cart but didn't buy.

Cart abandoners are the classic retargeting example. The audience itself takes two minutes to create. The part most people get wrong is the tracking underneath it, so this guide starts there.

Short answer: Make sure your website sends AddToCart and Purchase events to Meta. Then in Ads Manager go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Website, include people who triggered AddToCart in the last 14 days, exclude people who triggered Purchase in the same period, name it and click Create audience.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Check AddToCart and Purchase in Events Manager

    Open Events Manager, select your Pixel or dataset and look at the event list. You need AddToCart and Purchase in the last few days. InitiateCheckout and ViewContent are useful too.

    Test it yourself: open your store, add a product to cart, and check the Test events tab. You should see AddToCart appear.

    Important: If Purchase isn't tracked, the "didn't buy" part can't work, and you will show cart reminders to people who already paid. That annoys customers and wastes money.

  2. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  3. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Website and click Next.

  4. Include AddToCart

    Choose your Pixel under Source. Under Events, scroll to From your events and pick AddToCart. Set retention to 14 days to start.

  5. Exclude Purchase

    Click Exclude people. Choose the same Pixel, the event Purchase, and the same 14 days. The audience is now "added to cart, but didn't buy".

  6. Name it and create

    Name it like WEB | ATC no Purchase | 14d and click Create audience. Website audiences can look back up to 180 days, but cart intent fades fast, so keep this one short.

Audiences to build

6 cart and purchase audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

Added to cart, didn't buy, last 7 days

Setting: AddToCart 7d · exclude Purchase 7d
Name: WEB | ATC no Purchase | 7d

Your hottest group. Show the product they left, delivery details, or a reason to finish now.

02

Started checkout, didn't buy, last 7 days

Setting: InitiateCheckout 7d · exclude Purchase 7d
Name: WEB | IC no Purchase | 7d

Even closer to buying. Address checkout doubts: payment options, COD, returns, delivery time.

03

Added to cart, didn't buy, last 30 days

Setting: AddToCart 30d · exclude Purchase 30d
Name: WEB | ATC no Purchase | 30d

A larger, cooler version. Use for a reminder with reviews or a bundle.

04

Viewed product, no add to cart, last 14 days

Setting: ViewContent 14d · exclude AddToCart 14d
Name: WEB | VC no ATC | 14d

Browsers. Show best-sellers, reviews or the same product category.

05

Purchasers, last 180 days

Setting: Purchase · 180 days
Name: WEB | Purchase | 180d

Exclude from acquisition campaigns, or run a separate campaign for repeat orders and upsells.

06

Purchasers, last 30 days

Setting: Purchase · 30 days
Name: WEB | Purchase | 30d

Recent buyers. Exclude them from cart reminders, or ask for a review or referral.

Questions

Common doubts.

How do I create an add to cart but not purchased audience in Meta?

Create a Website Custom Audience, include people who triggered the AddToCart event in the last 14 days, then click Exclude people and exclude the Purchase event for the same period. Your store must send both events to Meta.

Why is my add to cart audience empty?

Usually the AddToCart event isn't reaching Meta. Check Events Manager for recent AddToCart events, and test by adding a product to cart yourself while watching the Test events tab.

What retention should I use for cart abandoners?

Start with 7 to 14 days. Purchase intent fades quickly. Website audiences can go up to 180 days, but longer windows are better for general reminders than cart recovery.

If I view a phone on a shopping site, will every brand retarget me on Instagram?

No. Only the website you visited can retarget you, and only if it has the Meta Pixel or Conversions API set up and you didn't block tracking. Your visit isn't shared with every advertiser.

Should I use Advantage+ catalog ads instead?

If you have a product catalog connected, catalog ads can show each person the exact product they viewed or added to cart. They use the same events, so the tracking checks in this guide still apply.
