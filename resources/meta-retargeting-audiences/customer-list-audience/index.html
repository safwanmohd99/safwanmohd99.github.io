Guide 7 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Use your own customer list in Meta Ads.

Your CRM, Google Sheet or billing software already has the people who know you best. Upload that list as a Custom Audience to stop paying to reach existing customers, to bring old customers back, or to find similar people.

Short answer: Prepare a CSV with email, phone (with country code) and name columns, only for people who agreed to hear from you. In Ads Manager go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Customer list, upload the file, check the column mapping, name it and click Import and create.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Prepare your file

    Export from your CRM or Sheet. Keep only the columns you need. More identifiers (email + phone + name + city) usually means more matches.

    emailphonefnlnctcountry
    anu@example.com919876543210AnuThomasKochiIN
    rahul@example.com971501234567RahulMenonDubaiAE

    Example data only, not real people.

    For clinics and hospitals: don't upload patient lists unless patients clearly agreed to marketing, and keep ads in line with NMC guidelines. Never put treatment or diagnosis details in the file or the audience name.

  2. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  3. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Customer list and click Next.

  4. Upload the file and accept the terms

    Meta asks you to accept the Custom Audiences terms the first time. Read and accept them. Then upload your CSV or paste the data, and give the audience a name like LIST | Customers | Nov 2026.

    Meta hashes (scrambles) the data in your browser before it is uploaded, and uses it only to find matching accounts.

  5. Check the column mapping

    Meta reads your headers and shows which column it thinks is email, phone and so on. Fix any column marked Do not upload that should be mapped, and fix errors like missing country codes. Then click Import and create.

  6. Wait for matching, then keep it updated

    Matching usually takes some time; Meta then shows the audience size. A customer list doesn't update on its own. Re-upload it every month or so, or use the Add customers option in the audience's menu.

Audiences to build

6 customer list audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

All customers

Setting: Full customer export · re-upload monthly
Name: LIST | All customers

Exclude from campaigns meant for new customers, so your budget goes to new people.

02

Past customers, no purchase in 6+ months

Setting: CRM filter: last purchase or visit older than 6 months
Name: LIST | Lapsed 6m+

Bring them back with a check-up reminder, a new service or a returning-customer offer.

03

Leads that didn't convert

Setting: CRM filter: enquired, not converted
Name: LIST | Leads not converted

Follow up with proof and answers to common objections. Often cheaper than finding new leads.

04

Best customers

Setting: Top customers by value or repeat visits
Name: LIST | Top customers

Use as the seed for a Lookalike audience. Meta needs at least 100 people from one country.

05

WhatsApp and phone enquiries

Setting: Numbers from WhatsApp Business or call logs (with consent)
Name: LIST | WA enquiries

People who enquired outside Meta's forms. Show them reminders or exclude them from lead ads.

06

Event or webinar attendees

Setting: Registration list
Name: LIST | Webinar Nov 2026

Follow up with the next step: a consultation, a demo, or the next event.

Questions

Common doubts.

Is it legal to upload my customer list to Meta?

It can be, if the people on the list gave you their details and agreed to marketing, and you follow local privacy law (in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act). You also accept Meta's Custom Audiences terms. Don't upload bought lists or data without consent.

What format should phone numbers be in?

Include the country code without spaces or symbols, for example 919876543210 for India or 971501234567 for the UAE. Numbers without a country code match poorly.

Why is my customer list match rate low?

Common reasons are missing country codes, old email addresses, or only one identifier per person. Add more columns like email, phone, name and city to improve matching.

What is the minimum size for a customer list audience?

Very small lists may not deliver ads. For a Lookalike audience, Meta needs a source audience of at least 100 people from one country.

Does a customer list update automatically?

No. It stays as you uploaded it. Re-upload or use Add customers when your list changes, or connect a CRM integration if your CRM supports it.
