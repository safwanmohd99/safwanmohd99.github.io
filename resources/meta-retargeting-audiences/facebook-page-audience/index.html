Guide 2 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who engage with your Facebook Page.

Many service businesses, clinics and B2B brands in Kerala and the GCC still get a lot of messages and calls from their Facebook Page. Here is how to turn those Page visitors and engagers into retargeting audiences.

Short answer: In Ads Manager, go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Facebook Page. Select your Page, choose an event such as "Everyone who engaged with your Page", set the retention in days, name it and click Create audience. No Pixel needed.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  2. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Facebook Page and click Next.

  3. Choose your Page and the event

    Select your Page under Source, then open Events. The options include:

    • Everyone who engaged with your Page
    • Anyone who visited your Page
    • People who engaged with any post or ad
    • People who clicked any call-to-action button (like Call now, Send message, Book now)
    • People who sent a message to your Page
    • People who saved your Page or any post

  4. Set the retention

    Type the number of days to look back. Meta shows the maximum next to the box; for Page engagement it is usually 365 days. For a hot audience use 30 days or less.

  5. Combine with Instagram if your audience is small (optional)

    Click Include more people, pick Instagram account and add the same kind of event. One audience then covers both platforms. Useful when each one alone is too small.

  6. Name it and click Create audience

    Example name: FB | Page visit | 90d. Click Create audience. It can take up to about 24 hours to fill.

Audiences to build

6 Facebook Page audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

All Page engagers, last 365 days

Setting: Everyone who engaged with your Page · 365 days
Name: FB | Engaged | 365d

Your widest Facebook warm pool. Good for trust-building content and offers you run all year.

02

Page visitors, last 90 days

Setting: Anyone who visited your Page · 90 days
Name: FB | Page visit | 90d

People who checked your Page, maybe after a referral or Google search. Show them reviews and a clear way to contact you.

03

Messaged your Page, last 180 days

Setting: People who sent a message to your Page · 180 days
Name: FB | Messaged | 180d

High intent. Use for a follow-up offer, or exclude them from cold campaigns.

04

Clicked a CTA button, last 90 days

Setting: People who clicked any call-to-action button · 90 days
Name: FB | CTA click | 90d

They tapped Call, Book or Message but may not have finished. A simple reminder ad works well here.

05

Post and ad engagers, last 60 days

Setting: People who engaged with any post or ad · 60 days
Name: FB | Post+Ad engaged | 60d

Follow up on a post that got attention with the next step: a case study, a demo, or an FAQ video.

06

Saved your Page or a post, last 180 days

Setting: People who saved your Page or any post · 180 days
Name: FB | Saved | 180d

Small but interested. Remind them later with seasonal offers or new services.

Questions

Common doubts.

What is a Facebook Page engagement Custom Audience?

It is a list Meta builds of people who interacted with your Facebook Page, such as visiting it, reacting to a post, clicking a button or sending a message. You can show ads to this list (retargeting) or exclude it from campaigns.

Can I retarget people who messaged my Facebook Page?

Yes. Choose the event "People who sent a message to your Page" when creating a Facebook Page Custom Audience. Many advertisers also exclude this group from campaigns aimed at new people.

Do Page likes or followers count as engagement?

Engagement covers actions like visiting the Page, reacting, commenting, sharing, clicking buttons and messaging. There is no separate "only people who liked my Page" option in Custom Audiences, so use "Everyone who engaged with your Page" for the widest group.

Should I create separate audiences for Facebook and Instagram?

Create them separately first so you can see the size of each. If both are small, combine them into one audience with Include more people.
