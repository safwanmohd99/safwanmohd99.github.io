The basics

Prospecting vs retargeting.

Two jobs, and most small advertisers only do the first one.

01

Prospecting: reaching new people

Ads shown to people who have never interacted with your business. This is how you grow, but strangers rarely buy or enquire on the first ad.

02

Retargeting: reconnecting with warm people

Ads shown to people who already know you: they watched a Reel, visited your profile, opened a form or browsed your website. In Meta Ads these groups are called Custom Audiences.

An example you've probably seen.

You check a phone on a brand's website, don't buy, and later see that brand's ad on Instagram. That is retargeting. It only happens when that website has the Meta Pixel or Conversions API set up and your browser allowed it. Not every product view turns into an ad.

Before you build

Five things to know first.

01

Where to find Audiences

Ads Manager → menu (☰ All tools) → Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience. In Meta Business Suite: All tools → Audiences.

02

Audiences need time and size

A new audience can take up to about 24 hours to fill. Very small audiences may not deliver, so combine sources or use longer windows if needed.

03

Different audiences, different messages

Someone who opened your form needs a different ad from someone who watched 3 seconds of a Reel. The campaign guide has a message-by-audience table.

04

Use exclusions

Exclude existing customers and recent leads from campaigns meant for new people. Run a separate campaign for upsells and repeat business.

05

Advantage+ can go beyond your audience

With Advantage+ audience, a Custom Audience is a suggestion, not a hard limit. Use the original audience options if you want to keep delivery to your audience.

Questions

Common doubts.

What is retargeting in Meta Ads?

Retargeting means showing ads to people who already interacted with your business, like watching your Reel, visiting your Instagram profile, opening your lead form or visiting your website. In Meta Ads you do this with Custom Audiences.

What is a Custom Audience in Meta Ads?

A Custom Audience is a group of people Meta builds from your own sources: your Instagram account, Facebook Page, videos, lead forms, website (through the Pixel) or an uploaded customer list. You can show ads to that group or exclude it from campaigns.

Where do I create Custom Audiences in Meta?

In Meta Ads Manager, open the menu and click Audiences, then Create audience and Custom audience. From Meta Business Suite, go to All tools and then Audiences.

Which retargeting audiences don't need a Meta Pixel?

Instagram account, Facebook Page, video and lead form audiences are built from activity on Facebook and Instagram, so they don't need a Pixel. Website and add-to-cart audiences need the Meta Pixel or Conversions API on your website.

How long does a Custom Audience take to be ready?

Usually a few hours. Meta says it can take up to 24 hours depending on the audience size and date range. Customer lists also need time for matching.

Will my ads only show to my Custom Audience?

Not always. With Advantage+ audience, Meta treats your Custom Audience as a suggestion and can show ads to other people too. Use the original audience options in the ad set if you want to keep delivery to that audience.
