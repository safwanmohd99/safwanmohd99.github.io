Guide 1 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who engage with your Instagram.

Everyone who visits your profile, likes a Reel, saves a post or sends a DM is already warmer than a stranger. Here is how to turn them into a Custom Audience in Meta Ads Manager, and the six Instagram audiences worth building first.

Short answer: In Ads Manager, go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Instagram account. Pick your professional account, choose who to include (for example, everyone who engaged), set how many days to look back, name it and click Create audience. No Pixel or website is needed.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  2. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Instagram account and click Next.

  3. Pick your Instagram account and the events

    Under Source, select your Instagram professional account. Then open the Events dropdown. You will see options like these:

    • Everyone who engaged with this professional account: the widest option, covering all the actions below.
    • Anyone who visited this professional account's profile
    • People who engaged with any post or ad: likes, comments, shares, saves and clicks.
    • People who sent a message to this professional account
    • People who saved any post or ad

    Start with one event per audience. Separate audiences are easier to understand later in your reports.

  4. Set the retention (how many days to look back)

    Retention is how far back Meta looks. 30 days means "people who engaged in the last 30 days". Meta shows the maximum next to the box; for Instagram engagement it is usually 365 days.

    Shorter windows give smaller but warmer audiences. Longer windows give bigger but colder ones. See the six audiences below for the windows I use.

  5. Add more rules if you need them (optional)

    Click Include more people to add another source in the same audience, for example your Facebook Page engagers. Click Exclude people to remove a group, for example people who already sent a message.

  6. Name it clearly and create it

    Use a name you will understand in six months. I use the pattern IG | Engaged | 30d: source, who, days. Add a short description if you like, then click Create audience.

    Give it a day. Meta needs time to fill the audience, up to about 24 hours depending on its size and the date range. The size shows in the Audiences list once it is ready.
Audiences to build

6 Instagram audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

All engagers, last 365 days

Setting: Everyone who engaged with this professional account · 365 days
Name: IG | Engaged | 365d

Your biggest warm audience. Use it for awareness-style retargeting: testimonials, "how we work" videos, behind-the-scenes.

02

Recent engagers, last 30 days

Setting: Everyone who engaged with this professional account · 30 days
Name: IG | Engaged | 30d

People who engaged recently remember you. Show them a clear offer or next step: book a call, WhatsApp us, visit the clinic.

03

Profile visitors, last 90 days

Setting: Anyone who visited this professional account's profile · 90 days
Name: IG | Profile visit | 90d

Visiting a profile means someone was curious enough to check you out. Good for your best proof: reviews, results, before-and-after where allowed.

04

People who sent a DM, last 180 days

Setting: People who sent a message to this professional account · 180 days
Name: IG | DM | 180d

Often your highest intent group. Either show them a reminder offer, or exclude them from cold campaigns because you already have the conversation.

05

People who saved a post or ad, last 90 days

Setting: People who saved any post or ad · 90 days
Name: IG | Saved | 90d

Saving means "I want to come back to this". Remind them with a related post, a guide, or a limited-time offer.

06

Post and ad engagers, last 60 days

Setting: People who engaged with any post or ad · 60 days
Name: IG | Post+Ad engaged | 60d

People who liked, commented or shared. Use for a second-step message after your Reel, like a deeper explainer or case study.

Questions

Common doubts.

Can I target my Instagram followers directly in Meta Ads?

Not as a follower list. Custom Audiences don't have a "followers only" option. The closest option is an Instagram account audience with "Everyone who engaged with this professional account" over a long window like 365 days. It covers people who visited, engaged or messaged, which includes most of your active followers.

Do I need a Meta Pixel for an Instagram engagement audience?

No. Instagram engagement audiences are built from activity on Instagram itself, so you don't need a website or Pixel. You only need an Instagram professional account connected to your ad account.

Why is my Instagram audience size showing as too small?

The account may have too little recent activity, or the retention window is short. Try a longer window (90 or 365 days), use "Everyone who engaged" instead of a narrow event, or add your Facebook Page engagers to the same audience with Include more people.

How long does it take for the audience to be ready?

Usually a few hours, and Meta says it can take up to 24 hours depending on the size and date range. After that, the audience keeps updating on its own as new people engage.

Is an Instagram engagement audience the same as retargeting?

It is one type of retargeting audience. Retargeting means showing ads to people who already interacted with your business. Instagram engagers are one group; video viewers, form openers and website visitors are others.
