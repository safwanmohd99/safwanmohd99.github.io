Guide 4 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who opened your form but didn't submit.

In lead ads, many people tap the button, open the Instant Form and then leave. They were close. A Lead form Custom Audience lets you bring them back with a second, better reason to fill it in.

Short answer: In Ads Manager, go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Lead form. Select your Page and form, choose "People who opened but didn't submit form", set retention (up to 90 days), name it and click Create audience.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  2. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Lead form and click Next.

  3. Choose your Page and the forms

    Select the Facebook Page under Source. Then choose which forms to include. Pick one form if it is for one service; pick all if you want one audience across forms.

  4. Pick the event

    Open Events. The options include:

    • Anyone who opened this form
    • People who opened but didn't submit form
    • People who opened and submitted form

    For retargeting, choose opened but didn't submit. Save the submitted version too, as an exclusion list.

  5. Set retention, name and create

    Lead form audiences have a shorter maximum: Meta usually allows up to 90 days. For "opened but didn't submit", 7 to 30 days works best because the interest fades quickly.

    Name it like LF | Opened not submitted | 30d and click Create audience.

    Don't see the "didn't submit" option? Meta changes menu labels from time to time. If your account only shows "opened" and "submitted", create the opened audience and exclude the submitted audience inside it using Exclude people.
Audiences to build

5 lead form audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

Opened but didn't submit, last 14 days

Setting: People who opened but didn't submit form · 14 days
Name: LF | Opened not submitted | 14d

The main one. Show a reminder with a new reason: a short testimonial, an answer to a common doubt, or a simpler offer.

02

Opened but didn't submit, last 30 days

Setting: People who opened but didn't submit form · 30 days
Name: LF | Opened not submitted | 30d

Same group with a longer window, for when the 14-day version is too small.

03

Submitted, last 90 days

Setting: People who opened and submitted form · 90 days
Name: LF | Submitted | 90d

Exclude this group from your lead campaigns so you don't pay to collect the same lead twice.

04

Anyone who opened, last 90 days

Setting: Anyone who opened this form · 90 days
Name: LF | Opened | 90d

A wider warm group. Useful for a different service or a later offer.

05

Openers of one service form, last 30 days

Setting: Opened · one specific form · 30 days
Name: LF | Hair transplant form | Opened | 30d

If you run forms for different services, keep separate audiences so each gets a matching follow-up ad.

Questions

Common doubts.

Can I retarget people who opened my Instant Form but didn't submit?

Yes. Create a Custom Audience with Lead form as the source and choose the event "People who opened but didn't submit form". If that option isn't shown in your account, create an "opened" audience and exclude people who submitted.

How long can a lead form audience look back?

Lead form audiences have a shorter limit than other engagement sources: Meta usually allows up to 90 days. Meta shows the exact maximum next to the retention box.

Why should I exclude people who already submitted the form?

If they already gave you their details, showing them the same lead ad wastes budget and can create duplicate leads. Follow up with them by phone or WhatsApp instead.

What ad should I show to people who didn't submit?

Give them a new reason, not the same ad again. A short video answering a common doubt, a client testimonial, or a simpler form with fewer questions often works better than repeating the first ad.

Does this audience need a Meta Pixel?

No. Instant Forms open inside Facebook and Instagram, so Meta records opens and submits without a Pixel on your website.
