Guide 8 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Turn your audiences into a retargeting campaign.

Building audiences is half the work. This guide covers the other half: setting up the ad set, understanding how Advantage+ audience treats your Custom Audiences, using exclusions, and showing each group the right message.

Short answer: Create a separate campaign or ad set for retargeting. In the ad set's Audience section, add your Custom Audiences, exclude people who already converted, and keep the message specific to that group. With Advantage+ audience on, Meta treats your Custom Audience as a suggestion and may show ads beyond it.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Decide: separate campaign or separate ad set

    Prospecting reaches new people. Retargeting reaches people who already interacted with you. Keep them apart so you can see the cost and results of each.

    For small budgets, one campaign with a separate retargeting ad set is fine. As budget grows, a separate retargeting campaign is easier to manage.

  2. Pick the objective

    Choose the objective that matches the action you want: Leads for forms, calls or WhatsApp chats, Sales for online purchases, Engagement for messages. Retargeting is not an objective by itself; it is who you show the ad to.

  3. Add your Custom Audiences in the ad set

    In the ad set, go to Audience. Meta's default is Advantage+ audience. Here, Custom Audiences you add work as an audience suggestion: Meta starts with them but may show ads to other people too if it expects better results.

    If you want the ads to stay with your warm audience, click Switch to original audience options (Meta may label it slightly differently), then add your Custom Audiences under Custom audiences.

    Keep in mind: Meta changes these settings often, and even with original options, things like Advantage+ placements or detailed targeting expansion can change delivery. Check the audience definition in the ad set before you publish.

  4. Exclude people who already converted

    In the same Audience section, under Exclude, add audiences like LIST | All customers, LF | Submitted | 90d or WEB | Lead | 180d. Do the same in your prospecting campaigns so you aren't paying to reach existing customers with "new customer" ads.

  5. Match the creative to the audience

    The biggest mistake is showing warm people the same ad they already saw. Use the table below as a starting point.

  6. Set a modest budget and watch frequency

    Retargeting audiences are small, so they need less budget than prospecting. Watch Frequency (how many times each person saw your ad) in Ads Manager. If it climbs high and results drop, refresh the creative or widen the audience.

Questions

Common doubts.

Is a Custom Audience a strict targeting restriction in Meta Ads?

Not always. With Advantage+ audience, a Custom Audience works as a suggestion and Meta may show ads beyond it. To keep delivery to your Custom Audience, use the original audience options in the ad set. Other settings can still affect delivery, so check the ad set before publishing.

Should retargeting be a separate campaign?

It doesn't have to be, but keeping retargeting in its own ad set or campaign makes it easier to see what it costs and what it brings in. With small budgets, one campaign with a separate retargeting ad set is fine.

How much budget should I give retargeting?

Less than prospecting, because the audiences are smaller. Start small, watch frequency and cost per result, and increase only if results hold up. There is no fixed percentage that suits every business.

What is the difference between prospecting and retargeting?

Prospecting shows ads to people who haven't interacted with your business yet. Retargeting shows ads to people who already have, such as video viewers, profile visitors, form openers or website visitors.

Should I exclude existing customers from my ads?

Usually yes for campaigns aimed at new customers, so your budget reaches new people. Run a separate campaign if you want to upsell, bring back past customers or ask for reviews.
