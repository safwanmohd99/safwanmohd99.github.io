Guide 3 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who watched your videos.

If someone watched 75% of your Reel, they are interested. Video audiences let you show them the next message instead of starting again with strangers. Here is how to build them in Ads Manager.

Short answer: In Ads Manager, go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Video. Choose how much people watched (for example, 50% of the video), select the Reels or video ads from your Facebook Page or Instagram account, set the retention in days, name it and click Create audience.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  2. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Video and click Next.

  3. Choose the engagement type

    Open the Engagement dropdown. Meta offers options based on how much of the video people watched:

    • People who have watched at least 3 seconds (big, but weak interest)
    • People who have completed ThruPlay or watched at least 15 seconds
    • People who have watched at least 25%, 50%, 75% or 95% of your video

    The higher the percentage, the more interested the person, and the smaller the audience.

  4. Choose the videos

    Click Choose videos. Switch between Facebook Page, Instagram account and Campaign at the top to find your videos. Tick the ones you want and click Confirm.

    Pick videos on one topic. Someone who watched your SEO Reel and someone who watched your hair treatment Reel need different follow-up ads.

  5. Set retention, name and create

    Set the retention in days (Meta usually allows up to 365 for video). Name it like VID | Retargeting Reels | 50% | 60d and click Create audience.

    Only new videos added later? A video audience includes only the videos you selected. When you post a new Reel on the same topic, edit the audience and add it.
Audiences to build

6 video audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

Hot viewers: 75% of any video, last 30 days

Setting: Watched at least 75% · all recent Reels · 30 days
Name: VID | All | 75% | 30d

Your warmest video audience. Show a direct offer or a "book a consultation" ad.

02

Strong interest: 50% viewers, last 60 days

Setting: Watched at least 50% · topic videos · 60 days
Name: VID | Topic | 50% | 60d

Good size and decent interest. Show a case study or a client testimonial on the same topic.

03

ThruPlay viewers, last 90 days

Setting: Completed ThruPlay or 15 seconds · 90 days
Name: VID | ThruPlay | 90d

A middle-ground audience for a second video that goes deeper into the topic.

04

Ad video viewers, last 30 days

Setting: Watched at least 50% · videos from one Campaign · 30 days
Name: VID | Campaign X | 50% | 30d

Build it from the videos in your prospecting campaign. This is classic two-step retargeting: video first, offer next.

05

95% viewers, last 365 days

Setting: Watched at least 95% · all videos · 365 days
Name: VID | All | 95% | 365d

People who watched nearly everything. Great as a seed for a Lookalike audience.

06

3-second viewers, last 14 days

Setting: Watched at least 3 seconds · 14 days
Name: VID | All | 3s | 14d

Big and cheap, but weak. Use only for a very light reminder, or skip it and use 25%+.

Questions

Common doubts.

What is ThruPlay in Meta Ads?

ThruPlay counts people who watched your video to the end if it is shorter than 15 seconds, or for at least 15 seconds if it is longer. In video audiences it is a middle option between 3-second views and percentage views.

Can I make an audience from Instagram Reels viewers?

Yes. In a Video Custom Audience, click Choose videos and switch to Instagram account to pick your Reels. The Instagram account must be a professional account connected to your ad account.

Which video percentage should I use for retargeting?

Start with 50% or 75%. 3-second viewers are a big audience but many of them scrolled past. Higher percentages give smaller audiences of people who actually paid attention.

Does a video audience update when I post new videos?

No. It only includes the videos you selected. Edit the audience and add new videos when you post more on the same topic.

How long can a video audience look back?

Meta shows the maximum next to the retention box; for video engagement it is usually 365 days.
