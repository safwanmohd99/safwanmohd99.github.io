Guide 5 of 8 · Meta Ads retargeting

Retarget people who visited your website.

Someone read your services page, checked the pricing and left. A website Custom Audience lets you reach them again on Facebook and Instagram. It only works if your website already sends data to Meta.

Short answer: First install the Meta Pixel (and Conversions API if you can). Then in Ads Manager go to Audiences → Create audience → Custom audience → Website. Select your Pixel, choose "All website visitors" or "People who visited specific web pages", set retention (up to 180 days), name it and click Create audience.

Step by step

How to set it up in Ads Manager.

Follow the steps in order. It takes about five minutes once your account is ready.

  1. Check the Pixel is working first

    Open Events Manager (business.facebook.com/events_manager), select your Pixel (Meta may call it a dataset) and look at the Overview tab. You should see PageView events from the last few days.

    No events? The audience will stay empty. Fix the Pixel first.

    Important: Not every website visit becomes part of a Meta audience. If the Pixel is missing, blocked by the browser or an ad blocker, or the visitor declined tracking, Meta can't record that visit. That is why website audiences are often smaller than your Google Analytics numbers.

  2. Open Audiences in Ads Manager

    Go to adsmanager.facebook.com and pick the right ad account at the top left. Open the menu (the ☰ All tools icon) and click Audiences.

    Working from Meta Business Suite instead? Click All tools in the left menu, then Audiences. It opens the same Audiences screen.

  3. Click Create audience, then Custom audience

    Click the blue Create audience button and choose Custom audience. Meta shows a list of sources. Choose Website and click Next.

  4. Choose the Pixel and who to include

    Under Source, pick your Pixel or dataset. Under Events, choose one of these:

    • All website visitors
    • People who visited specific web pages: then add a rule like URL contains /services/ or /pricing
    • Visitors by time spent: the top 5%, 10% or 25% most active visitors
    • From your events: people who triggered an event like Lead or Contact

  5. Exclude people who already enquired (recommended)

    Click Exclude people. Choose the same Pixel and the event Lead (or whatever event fires when someone submits your form). Now the audience is "visited services pages but didn't enquire".

    This only works if your form actually sends a Lead event. The Lead event section of the Pixel guide shows how.

  6. Set retention, name and create

    Website audiences go up to 180 days. Name it like WEB | Services page | no Lead | 30d and click Create audience.

Audiences to build

6 website audiences worth building.

These work for most businesses. Build the ones that match how people interact with you, and check the size of each before using it.

01

All visitors, last 30 days

Setting: All website visitors · 30 days
Name: WEB | All | 30d

Your basic website retargeting audience. Show your strongest proof and a clear contact option.

02

All visitors, last 180 days

Setting: All website visitors · 180 days
Name: WEB | All | 180d

Bigger and colder. Use for new services, seasonal offers or a reminder that you exist.

03

Service or pricing page, no enquiry, last 30 days

Setting: URL contains /services/ or /pricing · exclude Lead · 30 days
Name: WEB | Services | no Lead | 30d

They looked at what you sell but didn't contact you. Answer their likely doubts: cost, process, timeline.

04

Contact page visitors, no enquiry, last 14 days

Setting: URL contains /contact · exclude Lead · 14 days
Name: WEB | Contact | no Lead | 14d

They almost reached out. A WhatsApp or call ad with a simple message works well here.

05

Top 25% by time spent, last 30 days

Setting: Visitors by time spent · top 25% · 30 days
Name: WEB | Top 25% time | 30d

Your most engaged readers. Good for a consultation offer or a detailed case study.

06

Leads, last 180 days

Setting: From your events · Lead · 180 days
Name: WEB | Lead | 180d

Exclude them from acquisition campaigns, or use them as a Lookalike seed.

Questions

Common doubts.

Do I need a Meta Pixel to retarget website visitors?

Yes. Website Custom Audiences are built from data your website sends to Meta, through the Meta Pixel, Conversions API, or both. Without it, Meta doesn't know who visited.

Why is my website audience smaller than my Google Analytics visitors?

Some visits are never sent to Meta: ad blockers, browser privacy settings, visitors who declined tracking, or pages where the Pixel isn't installed. Meta also needs to match visitors to Facebook or Instagram accounts. Adding Conversions API usually helps.

How long can a website Custom Audience look back?

Up to 180 days.

Does every website visit or product view create a Meta retargeting audience?

No. Only visits on websites that have the Meta Pixel or Conversions API set up can be used, and only for that website owner's ad account. Visiting a big shopping site doesn't automatically put you in a Meta audience for other advertisers.

Can I retarget people who visited one specific page?

Yes. Choose "People who visited specific web pages" and add a rule like URL contains /pricing. You can add several pages in one audience.
