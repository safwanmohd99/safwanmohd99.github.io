How B2B is different

Ten good leads beat a hundred random ones.

B2B marketing sells to other businesses. Sales cycles are longer, deals are bigger, and the people searching are often managers comparing a few suppliers. Cheap leads don't help if your sales team can't close them.

So I focus on lead quality: Google Search for buyers who are already looking, service pages that rank for what they search, and Meta lead forms with questions that filter out people who aren't a fit.

Then I close the loop with your sales team, so budget moves to the keywords, markets and messages that bring real deals.

FAQ

B2B lead generation questions, answered.

What is B2B lead generation?

It's getting enquiries from businesses that might buy your product or service. For most B2B companies, the best sources are Google Search, an SEO-ready website with clear service pages, and targeted lead campaigns on Meta or LinkedIn.

Which channel works best for B2B leads?

Google Search usually brings the highest-intent B2B leads, because buyers search for suppliers by name and location. SEO builds that over time without paying per click. Meta lead campaigns can work for wholesale and SME buyers when the form has qualifying questions.

Do you work with companies in the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. I've worked with B2B brands in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar, including an HVAC contractor in Ras Al Khaimah and a commercial ice manufacturer in Riyadh. I work remotely from Kerala.

How do you measure lead quality?

By tracking which leads your sales team qualifies and closes, not just how many forms come in. That feedback decides which keywords, markets and ads get more budget.
