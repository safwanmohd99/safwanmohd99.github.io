What is B2B SEO, and why is it different?

B2B SEO means getting your company found on Google by other businesses that want to hire or buy from you. The searches are small and specific, like "hvac contractors in ras al khaimah", but each click can be a contract. For a contractor in the UAE, even two or three new clients in a month make a big difference.

So B2B SEO is not about traffic. It is about one clear page for each service and each emirate you serve, written the way facility managers, developers and procurement teams search, with a quick way to ask for a quote.

If you are comparing SEO companies in Dubai or the wider UAE: I'm not an agency. I'm a freelance SEO consultant who plans and does the work myself, and talks to you directly.

The lesson: you don't need a separate page for every way of saying the same thing. You need one strong page per service that covers the words your clients use, like "contractor", "company" and "AMC", and states plainly where you work.

Google shows different results by location, device and search history, so the order may look slightly different when you search. More examples are in my portfolio.

Should a UAE company start with Dubai or its own emirate?

Usually its own emirate first. Searches in Dubai are far more competitive in most B2B trades, with many established companies already ranking. A strong page in a smaller market brings clients sooner, and the same site then has the trust to compete in Dubai.

FAQ

B2B SEO in the UAE, answered.

Do you offer B2B SEO services in the UAE?

Yes. I plan and manage SEO for UAE companies that sell to other businesses, such as contractors, suppliers, manufacturers and service firms, remotely from Kerala, India. For example, I planned the website of IEC UAE, an HVAC contractor in Ras Al Khaimah, and its HVAC page ranks for "hvac contractors in ras al khaimah".

Are you an SEO company in Dubai?

No. I'm a freelance SEO consultant, not an agency, and I'm not based in Dubai. I work remotely with UAE companies, plan and do the SEO work myself, and talk to you directly on WhatsApp, Google Meet or email.

Why does B2B SEO get fewer clicks than other websites?

Because fewer people search for B2B services, but each one is a business that may hire you. That is why B2B SEO is judged by enquiries and new clients, not traffic. For a contractor in the UAE, even two or three new clients in a month make a big difference.

How long does B2B SEO take in the UAE?

Usually three to six months for steady results, depending on your emirate, your services and the state of your website. Specific searches in a smaller emirate often move faster than broad searches in Dubai. No honest consultant can promise a position on Google.

Do you work with companies in Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. The work is remote, so the emirate doesn't matter. I plan pages for the emirates and areas you actually serve.

Can you build the website as well?

Yes. I plan the structure and content and work with a small team of freelance developers to build it. For IEC UAE, I planned the whole website and the developers built it under my supervision.

Should I do SEO or Google Ads first?

If you need enquiries this month, start with Google Ads. If you want enquiries that keep coming without paying for every click, start SEO now, because it takes months. Many B2B companies run ads while SEO builds.
