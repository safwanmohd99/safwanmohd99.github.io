Why a consultation

An experienced second opinion, without a contract.

Most businesses don't need an agency on day one. They need someone to look at what they're already doing and say what's working, what's wasting money and what to do next.

FAQ

Consultation questions, answered.

What happens in a digital marketing consultation?

Before the call, I review your ads, website and Google profile. In the 1–2 hour session we go through what's working, what's wasting money and what to do next. After the call you get a clear action list you can run yourself or with your team.

How much does a consultation cost?

It's a one-time fee, priced on how much I need to review beforehand, such as the number of ad accounts, platforms and website pages. Message me on WhatsApp with a few lines about your business and I'll send a clear quote.

Can we talk in Malayalam?

Yes. I'm comfortable in both Malayalam and English.

Do I need to share my ad account?

View access helps a lot, because I can see real data instead of guessing. You can give view-only access to Meta Ads, Google Ads and Search Console and remove it after the session.

What if I need ongoing help after the consultation?

I take on full freelance projects only occasionally, when the fit is right. Most people run the action list themselves or with their team, and book another session later if they need it.
