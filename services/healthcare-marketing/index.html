Why healthcare is different

Patients choose on trust, not discounts.

Healthcare marketing isn't like selling a product. Patients compare doctors, read reviews and look for clear information before they book. And in India, doctors must follow the NMC's rules on advertising, which were updated on 6 October 2026.

Those rules ban things many agencies still do: patient testimonials, before-and-after photos, discounts and claims like "best doctor in Kochi". Meta and Google have their own health ad policies on top.

So I plan every campaign with both patient trust and the rules in mind: factual ads, helpful content, strong Google Maps presence, and tracking from the first click to the appointment.

FAQ

Healthcare marketing questions, answered.

Do you do digital marketing for clinics in Kerala?

Yes. Healthcare is the main focus of my work. I've worked with an allergy clinic network in Kerala, a doctor's Instagram in Kerala, hospitals and clinics in Bangalore, and a wellness centre in Hyderabad, across Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO and Google Maps.

Can doctors advertise on Facebook and Google in India?

Yes, within limits. Under the NMC advertising guidelines issued on 6 October 2026, doctors can share factual and educational information but cannot use testimonials, before-and-after photos, discounts, superiority claims or influencer promotion. Meta and Google also have their own healthcare ad policies.

Which works better for clinics, Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Google Ads works when patients already search for a treatment, like "root canal near me". Meta Ads works for awareness and for treatments people don't search for yet. Many clinics need both, plus a strong Google Maps profile.

How do you get more patients from Google Maps?

By keeping the Google Business Profile complete and accurate: the right categories, services, photos, timings and regular genuine reviews. For Niyati Women and Pain Center, the profile brought 327 calls and 255 direction requests in six months.

Do you handle patient data?

I don't need patient medical information to run campaigns. Leads go to your team, and any screenshots I share as case studies have personal details removed.
