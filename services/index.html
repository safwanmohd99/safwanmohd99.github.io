Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram ads)

Meta Ads are paid ads on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. They're ideal for reaching people who fit your ideal customer before they start searching, and for generating leads through instant forms or WhatsApp chats.

Most failed Meta campaigns aren't a targeting problem. They're a tracking, offer or lead-quality problem. I start there.

Example: for a group of allergy and asthma clinics in Kerala, moving from WhatsApp ads to lead forms with qualifying questions turned ₹49,634 of ad spend into ₹3,28,100 in revenue in one month. Read how the clinic switched from WhatsApp ads to lead forms, or see a ₹1,500 Meta Ads test for a dress manufacturer.

04

SEO, AEO & GEO (AI search optimisation)

SEO helps your website show up on Google and Google Maps. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same search index, so for Google it's the same work. AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) are newer names for making your pages the clearest answer to real questions, so AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini can describe your business correctly.

Nobody can guarantee a mention in an AI answer. But if your website and Google Business Profile don't explain clearly what you do and where, AI tools have little to go on.

Example: local SEO put Asker Sweets, a food manufacturer in Riyadh, at #1 on Google for samosa manufacturers in Riyadh, and Google's AI Overview names them for that search. My guide on how businesses get recommended by ChatGPT and Google AI Mode explains what actually matters.

FAQ

Digital marketing questions, answered.

What does a freelance digital marketer in Kerala do?

A freelance digital marketer plans and runs online marketing for businesses, usually Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO and social media, without the overhead of a full agency. I'm based in Palakkad and focus on B2B and healthcare businesses in Kerala, India and the GCC, mainly through consultations.

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your website rank in Google results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) are newer names for making your content the clearest answer to real questions and helping AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini describe your business correctly. For Google it's the same work: its AI Overviews and AI Mode use the normal search index, so good SEO comes first.

Should my business use Meta Ads or Google Ads?

Use Google Ads when people already search for what you offer, like "dentist near me". Use Meta Ads to reach people who aren't searching yet but fit your ideal customer. Many clinics and B2B companies use both: Google for high-intent demand, Meta for awareness and leads.

Can clinics and doctors in India run ads on Facebook and Google?

Yes, but only within strict limits. Under the NMC advertising guidelines issued on 6 October 2026, doctors cannot solicit patients or use testimonials, before-and-after photos, discounts, superiority claims or influencer promotion. Clinics and hospitals can share factual information and educational content, and Meta and Google have their own healthcare ad policies on top.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Usually three to six months for steady results, depending on competition and your website's current state. Local SEO with a well-optimised Google Business Profile can improve sooner.
