How I plan pages for a Saudi B2B website: an example

Most business websites in Saudi Arabia have a home page, an about page and one "Products" page. Buyers search for something much more specific. This is how I map searches to pages. The searches below are examples for an industrial supplier, not a client's data.

What the buyer searchesWhat they wantPage that answers it
"[product] supplier in saudi arabia"A company that can supply themA service page for that product, with areas served, minimum orders and a quote button
"[product] manufacturer in riyadh"A local maker, often to visit or pick upThe same page, plus a Google Business Profile with the right category and address
"best [product] manufacturers in saudi arabia"A shortlist to compareClear facts Google can quote: what you make, since when, capacity, certifications, who you supply
"how to choose a [product] supplier"Advice before buyingA helpful blog post that links to the service page

The third row is where AI Overviews appear. Google's AI picks businesses whose pages state plain, checkable facts. Vague lines like "we are the leading company" give it nothing to quote.

How long does SEO take in Saudi Arabia?

Usually three to six months for steady results, depending on how competitive your searches are and the state of your website today. Specific B2B searches, like a product plus "supplier in saudi arabia", often move faster than broad ones, because fewer websites answer them well. I can't promise a position on Google; nobody honest can. What I can show you every month is what changed in Search Console and what we are doing next.
