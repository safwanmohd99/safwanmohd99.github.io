What SEO means here

SEO, AEO and GEO are one job.

SEO (search engine optimisation) helps your website rank on Google and Google Maps. AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) are newer names for making your pages the clearest answer, so AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews can describe your business correctly.

For Google it's the same work: AI Overviews and AI Mode use the normal search index. So I start with the basics that still decide rankings: a clear site structure, one page per service, content that answers what customers actually search, and a well-kept Google Business Profile.

Nobody can promise a #1 ranking or a mention in an AI answer. What I can do is fix what's holding your site back and build the pages Google and AI tools need to understand you.

What's included

  • Technical SEO audit: indexing, speed, mobile, site structure and broken links
  • Keyword research grouped by intent, mapped to the right page
  • Service pages and blogs written around what customers really search
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile: categories, services, photos and reviews
  • Structured data (schema) that matches what's on the page
  • AI search checks: how ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews describe your brand
  • Monthly Search Console tracking: clicks, impressions, rankings and AI Overviews

FAQ

SEO, AEO & GEO questions, answered.

Are you a freelance SEO consultant in Kerala?

Yes. I'm based in Palakkad, Kerala, and I take on a few freelance SEO clients alongside my full-time job at a digital marketing agency in Bangalore. Most people start with a consultation where I review their website and Search Console, then we decide whether ongoing SEO work makes sense.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Usually three to six months for steady results, depending on your competition and the current state of your website. Local SEO with a well-kept Google Business Profile can improve sooner. New websites take longer because Google needs time to trust them.

Can you get my business into Google's AI Overviews or ChatGPT?

Nobody can guarantee it. AI Overviews use Google's normal search index, so good SEO comes first. Four of the brands I've worked with, including Bissan Ice and Asker Sweets, are named in Google's AI Overviews for their main searches, because their websites clearly explain what they do and where.

Do you do local SEO and Google Business Profile?

Yes. For clinics and local businesses, Google Maps often matters as much as the website. For Niyati Women and Pain Center, the Google profile brought 327 calls and 255 direction requests in six months, and the clinic ranks in the top 3 on Maps for its main searches in RR Nagar.
