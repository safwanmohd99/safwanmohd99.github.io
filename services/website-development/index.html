Two kinds of website

Static or custom: pick what you actually need.

A static website is a set of fast, simple pages with a contact form and WhatsApp button. It's secure, cheap to host and ideal for clinics, consultants and service companies.

A custom dynamic website has a backend: logins, booking, admin panels, databases and integrations. It's the right choice when you need more than pages and a contact form.

Either way, I plan the site around what your customers search for and ask, so it can rank on Google and turn visitors into enquiries, not just look good.

FAQ

Website development questions, answered.

Do you build websites in Kerala?

Yes. I'm based in Palakkad and work with a small team of freelance developers. We build static websites and custom dynamic websites with a backend, for businesses in Kerala, across India and in the GCC.

Should I choose a static or a dynamic website?

Choose static if you mainly need service pages, a contact form and fast loading. Choose dynamic if you need logins, booking, an admin panel, a database or integrations. I'll recommend the simpler option if it does the job.

Will my new website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site is planned for SEO from the start: one page per service, clean structure, fast loading, titles and meta descriptions, structured data, and Search Console set up at launch.

Do you build ecommerce websites?

No. I focus on websites for service businesses, clinics and B2B companies that want enquiries, not online stores.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and the features you need. Message me on WhatsApp with what you need and I'll send a clear quote before anything starts.
